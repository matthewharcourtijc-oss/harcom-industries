Rook’s Field Companion

A field manual that does not depend on anything else. When you are deep in the woods and the phone shows no bars, it opens, it works and it keeps your records safe.

Built for UK woodland

Reference material and tools aimed at the conditions you actually meet on British ground.

Reliability first

Our one non-negotiable rule. Every feature has to work with no connection before it ships.

Yours alone

Nothing is sent to a server. What you write on the device stays on the device.

Everything in the kit

Simple tools that earn their place in the pack.

Tasks

Keep a running list of what needs doing, and tick it off as the day goes on.

Notes

Write down what you see, find and decide, without needing a connection to save it.

Mission log

A running record of the day, in order, so you can look back on what happened and when.

Offline storage

Everything is saved locally on your device, so there is nothing to sync and nothing to lose to a dropped signal.

How it works

Three steps, and none of them need a network.

  1. 1

    Open it

    Launch the app. There is no sign-up, no login and no loading screen waiting on a server.

  2. 2

    Use it in the field

    Add tasks, write notes and log what happens as it happens.

  3. 3

    Keep it with you

    Your information is stored on your own device, ready next time you head out.

How we build

Our operating standard applies across the portfolio.

01 · CONTROL

Human authority

People decide what gets built, what changes and what ships. AI may execute assigned work; it does not grant itself authority.

02 · PROTECT

Security by design

Use least privilege, protected branches, isolated workspaces, secret separation, recoverable backups and auditable changes.

03 · VERIFY

Evidence before claims

Check, test, fix and test again. Distinguish automated checks from real-device validation and independent security review.

Questions

The short answers.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. It is designed to work offline from the start. A connection is never required to use it.

Where is my data kept?

On your own device. We do not run a cloud service for it, so there is no account and nothing is uploaded.

Is it only for woodland?

It is built around UK woodland, but the tools for tasks, notes and logging are useful anywhere you work without signal.

How do I follow updates?

Watch the project on GitHub. Stable and in-development versions are kept on separate branches.

Privacy

The app

Rook’s Field Companion is offline-first. The tasks, notes and log entries you create are stored on your own device. We do not run a cloud service for them, we do not require an account and we do not receive your data.
