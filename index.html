HARCOM portfolio

A clear view of the products, engineering systems and research programmes. Live releases are separated from development, private work and future ideas. No project is described as finished when it is not.

Rook’s Field Companion

An offline-first field companion with tasks, notes, mission logs, UK countryside reference material and local backups. The public release is v0.9.9. Browser data is stored locally and is not encrypted; backups and recovery improvements remain central to ongoing engineering.

Android development · v0.7.4

HARCOM Vault

Private native Android encrypted storage using Android Keystore integration and recovery archives. The repository contains development source, not a finished public release. Build verification, physical-device validation, signed release work and independent security review remain outstanding. No certification is claimed.

Source recovery required

Paradox Vault

A separate vault programme. Its original source archive has not yet been recovered into the repository, so the project is not currently buildable and no installable application exists. Source recovery and integrity review come before implementation.

Engine frozen · review pending

Viking AI Council

A multi-perspective AI council with distinct Optimist, Sceptic and Pragmatist voices, a Chair, REAL/FALLBACK health reporting and human approval. The v1 foundation and later chair-analysis work are preserved. The engine remains parked while run repeatability and fallback behaviour are reviewed; human approval remains the final gate.

Private · draft / under review

HARCOM Engineering Control Centre

A private engineering oversight dashboard for approved HARCOM projects. Its direction is governed automation: read-only monitoring, competing analysis, approval requests, controlled actions and independent evidence. It is not a public service; production changes and releases remain under Viking’s authority.

Discovery & architecture

Rook Forge

The foundation for Rook Standalone 2.0, an independent Android companion targeting the Samsung Galaxy S22. The design priorities are offline-first operation, local user-controlled memory, voice and clear permission boundaries. It remains in discovery and architecture; no finished app or APK is claimed.

In design

Void Writers

A proposed four-agent engineering workflow: Architect, Writer, Checker and Fixer. The model centres on separate responsibilities, restricted tools, independent checking and human-controlled releases. It remains a design programme.

Future programme

OFFGRID

A longer-term offline Rook companion concept exploring local AI, speech, persistent memory and optional connected operation. Planned as a future programme after the current foundations are stable; not yet in active build.

Long-term research

Project Zeus

HARCOM’s long-range research ambition for an original AI model, including architecture, training, evaluation and inference. This is a research goal, not an existing trained model.

How we operate

Reliability is the product

The Rook Engineering Protocol guides how work moves from idea to release.

01 · CONTROL

Human authority

Viking retains final approval. AI agents operate within explicit permissions and cannot grant themselves authority.

02 · PROTECT

Security by design

Least privilege, separate development and production identities, recoverable backups and auditable changes.

03 · VERIFY

Evidence before claims

Independent checks and honest reporting. A successful automated check is not the same as a real-device test or a security audit.
