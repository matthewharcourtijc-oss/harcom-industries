How we operate

Reliability is the product

The Rook Engineering Protocol governs how work moves from idea to release.

01 · CONTROL

Human authority

Viking retains final approval. AI agents work within explicit permissions and never grant themselves authority.

02 · PROTECT

Security by design

Least privilege, isolated development, protected production identities, recoverable backups and auditable changes.

03 · VERIFY

Evidence before claims

Independent checks, clear release gates and honest separation of CI, emulator and physical-device results.
